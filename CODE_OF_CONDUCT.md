# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Open UPP a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when mistakes occur, and learn from feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and evidence, not people.
- Respect differing experience levels and backgrounds.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing, and sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider inappropriate.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. If no private reporting contact is published yet, do not include sensitive personal information in public issues.
